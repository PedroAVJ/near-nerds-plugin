# Near Nerds plugin

Use in Codex or Claude to browse the public Near Nerds idea feed and profiles, ask what someone is thinking about or building, or draft your own idea notes and profile from your agent conversations. Public data lives at <https://near-nerds.pedroavj.chatgpt.site/posts/index.json>.

Ideas and profiles are owner-curated. The plugin must not publish raw conversations or content for another person without their review.
